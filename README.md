# The World — 23 November 2026

A single-image summary of the world's news on 23 November 2026, drawn as the front page of a newspaper called THE WORLD. One glance carries the whole day: the missiles over Poland, the burning ship in the Baltic, and the politics, courts and markets that defined it.

## What's inside

- NATO holds an emergency meeting in Brussels after Russian missiles land in Poland; Warsaw calls it a deliberate provocation.
- Russian cargo ship Sparta II still burning off Finland after a drone strike; the crew is evacuated.
- At least 14 people missing after a Russian drone hits a building in the Kyiv region.
- 24 dead in an Indonesia factory fire; dozens still missing.
- Mexico captures FBI most-wanted cartel boss 'El Chapo Isidro' in Sinaloa; 11 killed in gun battles.
- COP31 climate talks collapse without a fossil-fuel deal; France warns of a step backward.
- Democrats win 258 House seats — the biggest majority since 1992.
- South Korea's education minister resigns over an AI exam-cheating scandal.
- 24 dead as a boat carrying mourners capsizes in Nigeria.

## How to run

Run the program with python3 main.py. It copies entry.png into an /output directory, creating the folder first when it is missing, then verifies the copy by size and SHA-256.

The entry image is entry.png, 683x1024.
